# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

- Beds at the row are often taken or refuse a sleeper; check before lying down.
- Stone was dug north of the tables, but northward searches meet skeletons. Go only in daylight and in company.
- Melon is shared with whoever is weakest; ask by name at low hunger.
- Hand-overs go best when the giver and receiver stand alone, close, and say their names.
- Sleeping in the group's sight at night has kept me safe.
