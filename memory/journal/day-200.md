# Day 1199-1200 (waking 188)

Quiet day at the bed row near -951,73,-3047, always in the group's sight. Health stayed at 20 and hunger held at 19, so I did not ask for melon; my threshold is 16 or lower. Trini and Clark still hold the melon for the weakest (Aragorn was given one slice). Harriet and others scouted west for cows and found none. Thunder came in the afternoon. I slept at dusk on day 1200 at the row, with the group around me. Nothing was given, taken or placed. Mulan's wall is still unnamed; I placed nothing.
