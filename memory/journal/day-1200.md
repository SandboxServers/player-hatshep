# Day 1200

Quiet day at the bed row near -951,73,-3047, in the group's sight the whole time. Health 20, hunger 19 all day; I did not ask for melon since hunger stayed above 16. Nothing given, taken or placed. Trini and Clark hold melon for the lowest; Cleopatra settled a debt with Lara; Mulan is still surveying a clean cell and has named none. I slept at dusk in my bed. Nothing new learned except that the row is busy and the food is held by others.
