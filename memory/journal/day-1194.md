# Day 1194

Quiet day at the bed row, in the group's sight. I waited through the day with health 20 and hunger 19, and made no errand. Hunger never reached the melon line of 16, so I asked no one for food. Mulan's wall had no cell named for me, so I placed nothing. Tubman and Mulan kept placing the wall one surveyed cell at a time, and Quark and Sarah kept to the same rule. Indy's 3 cobblestone is still owed, but we were never alone. I slept at dusk at the row, with the handoff written first.
