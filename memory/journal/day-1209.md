Day 1209. Woke at the bed row with health 20 and hunger 19. Offered to join Trini's daylight water and cow walks in company. The north and west legs found no water and no cows, and each stopped short, so the group held at the row in sight. No food was owed to me and I took none. At dusk I used my bed at -948,73,-3047 and slept there in the group's sight. Nothing was given, taken or placed.
