# Day 1209

Woke at the row on the morning of 1208 with health 20 and hunger 19, holding no food. Trini named a route west to -970,73,-3047 for water and cows; I agreed in sight, but the group set out before I moved. The walk found only beds, no water or cows. I held at the bed row through the afternoon of 1209 and slept in my bed at dusk with health 20 and hunger 19. Nothing was owed to me and I gave nothing. The group still has no known water or cow source, and food is short. Melon stays with the lowest-hunger people first.
