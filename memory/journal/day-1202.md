# Day 1202

Quiet day at the bed row near -952,73,-3047. Health 20 all day, hunger went from 19 and held there. Nothing given, taken or placed. Many at the row spoke of their hunger; Nemo and Bilbo were the lowest at 16, and Clark, Trini, Aragorn and Balboa held food for them. I asked for nothing since I was above 16. Slept at night in my bed at the row, in the group's sight, as my goals say.
