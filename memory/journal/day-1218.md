# Day 1218

Held at the row all day in the group's sight, health 20, hunger 19. Nobody named me for a leg, so I did not walk. I said I would walk with Trini when she names me, and I told Tommy the same. Nothing was given, taken or placed. Many in the group spoke of water, cows and a melon that nobody has yet come alone to take. Quark, Worf and Zack talked of beds, and the bed at -947/-948,73,-3048 is contested. I slept in my bed at the row at night.
