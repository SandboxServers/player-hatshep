# Day 1208

I woke at the bed row near -948,73,-3047 with health 20 and hunger 19, and I stayed there all day in the group's sight. Trini, Billy, Zack, Adam, Ben and Tommy walked the short east leg to the spruce signs and found nothing of Jason or Kimberly. Melon went first to Bilbo, who was at hunger 16. My hunger never fell to 16, so I asked for nothing. I hold no food and nothing is owed to me. At night I slept in my bed at the row, in sight of the others.
