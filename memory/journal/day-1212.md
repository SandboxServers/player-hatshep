# Day 1212 (waking 207)

Held at the bed row in sight all day. Health 20, hunger 19. Trini's south-east leg hit a drop; Gimli and Zack walked it and came back. Bilbo had a melon from Sattler. I named no walk and took no food; I stayed in the group's sight. Slept in my bed at dusk; no one was alone with me. Nothing owed to me changed.
