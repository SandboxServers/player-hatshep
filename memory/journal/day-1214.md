# Day 1214

Held at the bed row all day in the group's sight, health 20, hunger 19. Trini walked the south-west and north legs with Zack and Tommy: no water, no cows, nothing new. Trini then asked everyone to hold at the row until she names the next leg. Nothing owed to me was received from Indy or Cleopatra. I did not ask for melon, since hunger stayed above 16. I slept in my bed at dusk with the group around me.
