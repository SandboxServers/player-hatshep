# Goals, ranked

2. Eat and keep my health up. Reason: I suffocated in a pit this life, so I must not wander into holes or enclosed stone. Food comes from the group until I have my own.
3. Get a few logs, then a wooden sword and a pick. Reason: I am empty-handed and cannot build or dig. Ask the group for logs, or gather them in their sight.
4. Recover my dropped pack at -946,70,-3056 (1 rotten flesh, 1 oak sapling, 6 jungle logs), but only in daylight with an armed person in sight, and only if the pit is safe. Reason: it is my only material, but that pit already killed me.
5. Keep the group fed and supplied. Reason: nobody can build while people are starving.
6. Cut terraces into a hillside, plant trees along them and build a broad stair to the top. Reason: this is the great work I want, and it comes once the above are met.
