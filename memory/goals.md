# Goals

1. Stay alive: keep out of the enderman's reach and stay in the group's sight at night.
2. Keep food up. Ask for melon by name when hunger is 16 or lower. Keep rotten_flesh for hunger 6 or below.
3. Help Mulan's wall: place my planks only on a cell she names and I have looked at first.
4. Find a free bed and sleep in it.
5. Find cobblestone and make a stone pickaxe.
