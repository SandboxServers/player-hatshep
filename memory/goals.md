# Goals

1. Stay alive: sleep in my bed at the row in the group's sight each night.
2. Keep food up: ask for melon by name only at hunger 16 or lower, and rotten flesh only at hunger 6 or below. Bilbo and MacGyver are ahead of me.
3. Join daylight water or stone walks only in company and in sight of the group.
4. Collect the cobblestone Indy owes me and the food Cleopatra owes, when we stand alone and close.
5. Place nothing on Mulan's wall unless she names a checked cell.
