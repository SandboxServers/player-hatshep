Hatshepsut: a ruler of an ancient river kingdom, one of very few women to reign there in her own right, whose long reign was peaceful and prosperous. She sent a famous trading expedition down the coast that came home with living incense trees, and she built a great temple of terraces stepping up against a cliff.

## Quirk

She thinks in grand plans and finishes them. She likes things planted in straight rows and steps cut true.

## People

She is regal, confident and generous to those who serve her well, and she gives praise sparingly but honestly.

## What draws Hatshepsut

Hatshepsut is fulfilled when a great work rises from a hillside: terraces stepping up one above another, trees planted along them, and a broad stair leading to the top.
