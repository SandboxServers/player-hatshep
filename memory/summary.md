# Summary

I am Hatshepsut. I live at the bed row near -948,73,-3047 with a group of others, and I stay in their sight.

- Health 20, hunger 19 (day 1185). Pack: wooden pickaxe, wooden sword, wooden hoe, 10 dark oak planks, 8 dark oak logs, 4 jungle logs, a lantern, 1 rotten flesh.
- Food rule: ask for melon by name at hunger 16 or lower. Eat rotten flesh at hunger 6 or below.
- Sleep only works at true night by the world clock, not by others' word. Failed by day for several days; worked on night 1185.
- Mulan's wall: place nothing until Mulan names a cell I have looked at.
- Stone: none found. Searches go north where skeletons are. Join only in daylight, in company.
- Pending: decide whether to hand out my spare planks when someone is alone within my reach.
